Section 01

📋 Introduction

Welcome to Smart Ledger — Your Pocket Accounting System. This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Ledger ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

Smart Ledger is designed as a fully offline, local-data accounting application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring complete transparency about how your data is handled.

By downloading and using Smart Ledger, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the application.

Section 02

🔒 Information We Do NOT Collect

✓ Smart Ledger collects absolutely NO personal data

Smart Ledger operates entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Financial data, journal entries, account balances, or transaction records
  • Device identifiers, IMEI, or hardware information
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Usage statistics or behavioral analytics
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data sent to our servers
  • Contact lists, camera, or microphone data
Section 03

📱 How the App Works

Smart Ledger is a completely offline accounting system. Here is exactly how your data is handled:

  • Local Storage Only: All journal entries, ledger accounts, transactions, and financial reports are stored exclusively on your device using local device storage.
  • No Internet Required: The app functions fully without an internet connection. No data is ever sent to external servers.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: We do not integrate any analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking libraries.
  • No Cloud Backup: Your data is not automatically backed up to any cloud service by Smart Ledger.
  • You Control Your Data: Export your data as PDF or Excel at any time. Import data from previously exported PDF reports.
Section 04

⚙️ App Features & Data Handling

Smart Ledger provides the following features, all processed locally on your device:

  • Journal Entries: Record double-entry transactions with debit and credit lines — stored locally only.
  • Auto-Generated Reports: Ledger, Trial Balance, Income Statement, and Balance Sheet are generated automatically from your local journal entries.
  • PDF & Excel Export: Reports are generated and saved to your device's local storage or shared via your device's share function. No data is sent to our servers.
  • Data Import: PDF reports exported from Smart Ledger can be re-imported to restore data — all processed locally.
  • 25 Currencies: Currency selection (USD, EUR, GBP, PKR, etc.) is a display preference stored locally.
  • Data Clear: Deleting data permanently removes all records from your device. This action is irreversible — always export your data before clearing.

⚠️ Important: Clearing data in Smart Ledger permanently deletes all your financial records from your device. We strongly recommend exporting your data as PDF or Excel before using the Clear Data function.

Section 05

🌐 Internet & Permissions

Smart Ledger may request the following device permissions, used solely for the described purposes:

  • Storage / Files Access: Required to save exported PDF and Excel reports to your device's Downloads folder and to import previously exported data files.
  • No Internet Permission: Smart Ledger does not require or use internet connectivity for any core functionality.

No permissions are used to collect, transmit, or share your personal or financial data.
